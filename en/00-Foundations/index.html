Branch 00 · The Common Foundation

Foundations of Chemistry: Where Every Branch Begins

Wherever you go next—organic, physical, or biochemistry—you cannot escape one shared language: atoms—chemical bonds—reactions—solutions. This branch uses 4 chapters to lay these common foundations on solid ground. It is designed for learners starting from zero or with a shaky background— every concept sets out from an everyday phenomenon, paired with interactive animations and misconception smashers.

4 chapters · about 3 hours Prerequisites: none (starting branch) 20 self-test questions Interactive animations ×5

🎯 What you will learn in this branch

  • Chapter 1 · Atoms and Elements—Journey inside the atom: how protons, neutrons, and electrons decide "who you are, what charge you carry, and how heavy you are," plus a first look at the periodic table.
  • Chapter 2 · Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure—Why atoms "hold hands": ionic, covalent, and metallic bonds; why molecules have shapes; why hydrogen bonds make water so special.
  • Chapter 3 · Chemical Reactions and Stoichiometry—Learn the chemist's arithmetic: balancing equations, the mole, ratio calculations, and the limiting reactant.
  • Chapter 4 · States of Matter and Solutions—Particle kinetic energy and the three states, the gas laws, ways to express concentration, and dilution calculations.

After finishing, you will be able to: understand what any chemical formula or equation is telling you; use the "mole" to convert freely between the world of atoms and grams; and grasp the tools that every later branch reaches for again and again—the periodic table, molecular shapes, and solution concentration.

🗺️ Prerequisites and study path

This branch requires no prerequisites at all. Simply work through chapters 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 in order; it works best to score above 80% on each chapter's self-test before starting the next. A suggested weekly rhythm: one chapter a day, with day 5 set aside for reviewing your mistakes.

📌 After completing this branch
You can continue into any of the "five major chemistry" branches (01 Inorganic / 02 Organic / 03 Analytical), all of which name this branch as their sole or main prerequisite.

🧩 Core concept map: four interlocking rings

  • Atoms (Chapter 1) answer "what the building blocks look like"—elements, isotopes, ions.
  • Chemical bonds (Chapter 2) answer "how the blocks fit together"—the electronegativity difference sets the bond type, VSEPR sets the shape, and intermolecular forces set boiling point and solubility.
  • Reactions and stoichiometry (Chapter 3) answer "how to settle the accounts after building"—conservation of mass and mole ratios.
  • States of matter and solutions (Chapter 4) answer "what happens when vast numbers of molecules gather"—the quantitative world of temperature, pressure, and concentration.
